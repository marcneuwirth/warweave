# ADR-0050 — Only unit purchases generate affinity

**Status:** accepted · **Ticket:** [#19](https://github.com/marcneuwirth/warweave/issues/19) · **Spec:** §6.1

## Context

`branchInvestment` counted squads **and** technologies. That was sound at 2 choices / 1 active but breaks at the expansion's 4 choices / 2 active shape, where a player buys four technologies for one unit type, runs two, and banks 1,000 gold of affinity for two purchases with no gameplay effect. The invariant written to bound that ([ADR-0015](0015-per-unit-type-technology-investment-cap.md)) caps the pump rather than removing it.

The tier-3 row makes the same question live again in v0.4, since upgrade tracks are a third purchasable layer.

## Decision

> **Only unit purchases generate affinity.** Technologies and track steps generate none.

This supersedes the earlier rule for technologies.

## Why removal rather than a cap

**It closes the pump by removing the mechanism instead of bounding it.** A cap has to be maintained against every future pool shape; a rule that non-unit purchases simply do not count survives any pool shape, including ones nobody has designed yet.

It also makes affinity mean something cleaner: **affinity describes the squads you have on the field**, which is what the affinity system exists to do and what the currently-owned rule ([ADR-0012](0012-affinity-tracks-currently-owned.md)) was already reaching for. Under the old rule a player could hold affinity generated by purchases that consume no slot and appear nowhere in the army.

## Consequences

- The per-unit-type technology investment cap becomes **dormant** — retained as a forward invariant that goes live the moment any non-unit purchase counts again.
- Reaching an affinity gate now always costs **squad slots**, which restores the tension the slot-free technology path had quietly removed: the slot-optimal path to Affinity 5 is no longer three squads plus two technologies.
- Track steps being affinity-neutral is what lets them be priced purely against the slot ceiling ([ADR-0049](0049-repricing-against-the-slot-ceiling.md)) without a second effect to reason about.
